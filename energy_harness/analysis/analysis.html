The energy of speculative decoding is architecture-dependent

Full 168-point sweep: 4 models × speculation length γ∈{0..5} × concurrency C∈{8..512}, run to completion, greedy, RedHat EAGLE-3 heads, GPU-package energy from the MI300X hardware counter (Zeus). All quantities below are derived in this page's JS from the raw measured columns — J_per_tok, window_throughput_tok_s, avg_power_W, acceptance_rate, mean_accepted_len — deduped by run_tag (last write wins).

Caveats (read before citing): single measurement per point (no error bars yet). The Llama-70B high-C points are mildly KV-limited (32k context cap → mean_running < C at C512; γ0 J/tok non-monotonic C256→512), so its high-C magnitudes are soft. Llama-8B C8/C16 have a finite-pool drain artifact (effective C ≈ 5.7 at C8). MFU (Fig 5) uses the bf16 peak (1.307 PFLOP/s) for all models; gpt-oss is MXFP4 but MI300X has no native FP4 → dequant-emulated, so bf16 peak is the right denominator, pending exact code-trace of the dequant path. It is the cross-model MFU ratio and its trend, not the absolute value, that carries the mechanism argument.
